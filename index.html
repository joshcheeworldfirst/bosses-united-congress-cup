Bosses United in sixty seconds

“Football is our passion. Business is our mission.”

Bosses United is a Singapore football club founded in 2022 whose players are all business owners — a club that mixes passion with mission, using the game to help owners stay active, form genuine friendships, share knowledge and give back. Grown out of the Bosses Network Young Chapter, it now counts 150+ members playing organised fixtures and overseas tours, built on values of dedication, discipline and unity — the friendships formed on the pitch carry into business.

If asked

Quick answers

How big is Bosses United?
150+ members and counting, across all chapters. Never quote a smaller number — the businesses we profile are just the recognisable names, not the community's size.
Are we the “official” or “preferred” payment partner?
Official payment partner of Bosses United — confirmed, signed 7 August.
What has the partnership actually done?
Since 13 July: a 100+ guest film screening, a friendly match, a signing ceremony at Wheeler's Estate, and ten filmed founder interviews. The Cup runs this week; monthly clinics start October.
Where's the cross-border angle?
Documented, not assumed — rice imports from nine countries, ASEAN subsidiaries, regional recruitment. The businesses section above has the names.
Why is Korea represented?
It's the one market with no existing member link — treat it as a forward-looking corridor rather than claiming a connection.
