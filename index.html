Our partner

Bosses United in sixty seconds

“Football is our passion. Business is our mission.”

Bosses United, founded in Singapore in 2022, is a community of business owners, CEOs and senior business leaders across Asia who train, compete and grow together in football. Grown out of the Bosses Network Young Chapter, it now counts 150+ members playing organised fixtures, company visits and overseas tours, built on values of dedication, discipline and unity — the friendships formed on the pitch carry into business. They have grown into six chapters — Malaysia, Thailand, Japan, South Korea, China and Hong Kong — and are aggressively expanding in the coming year.
