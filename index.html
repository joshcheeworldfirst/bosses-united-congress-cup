▮ Who we're partnering

Bosses United in sixty seconds

A competitive football team formed in 2022 from the Bosses Network Young Chapter — 150+ business owners and counting, meeting weekly with a shared goal. Networking and community are the purpose; football is the mechanism. Their tagline, from the team bus: “Football is our passion. Business is our mission.”

The football is real: organised fixtures, tours to Bangkok (2023) and Hong Kong (2025), time on the pitch with Abbas Saad and a meeting with Keisuke Honda. Spokesperson: Albert Ng.

In 2025 they signed an MOU with the Hong Kong Amateur Football Association — written wider than football, linking both cities' business communities. Their initiative. Credit it.

▮ If you're asked

Quick answers

How many members does Bosses United have?

150+ and counting. Names like Tong Seng Produce, TEHC and Jack's Place are the recognisable tip, not the total.

Why is WorldFirst partnering a football team?

Every player is an owner or director. We're not buying attention — we're joining a room that convenes our exact customer, weekly, by choice.

What have we done together so far?

Four touchpoints since 13 July: the ‘Dear You’ screening (100+ clients), the SAFRA friendly, the partnership signing at Wheeler's Estate, and a ten-founder interview series.

Where's the cross-border business?

Documented, not assumed — rice imports from nine countries, ASEAN subsidiaries, regional recruitment. The businesses section has the specifics.

What's the Hong Kong link?

Their own MOU with the Hong Kong Amateur Football Association, signed after their 2025 tour — deliberately wider than football.

Are we the “official” or “preferred” payment partner?

Until it's confirmed against the signed agreement, say “payment partner of Bosses United”, unqualified.

What's next after the Cup?

The exhibition match on 28 Aug, the closing ceremony and trophy on 30 Aug, then monthly clinics October–December — 20 curated seats each.
