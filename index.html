If asked

Quick answers

How big is Bosses United?
150+ members and counting. Never quote a smaller number — the 40-odd businesses we profile are just the recognisable names, not the community's size.
Are we the “official” or “preferred” payment partner?
Until it's confirmed against the signed agreement, say “payment partner of Bosses United”, unqualified.
What has the partnership actually done?
Since 13 July: a 100+ guest film screening, a friendly match, a signing ceremony at Wheeler's Estate, and ten filmed founder interviews. The Cup runs this week; monthly clinics start October.
Where's the cross-border angle?
Documented, not assumed — rice imports from nine countries, ASEAN subsidiaries, regional recruitment. The businesses section above has the names.
Why is Korea represented?
It's the one market with no existing member link — treat it as a forward-looking corridor rather than claiming a connection.
